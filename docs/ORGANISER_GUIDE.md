# Casa Atleta — organiser guide (admin area)

Sign in at **casaatleta.com/admin/login** with the email and password your hosting person created for you.

There are two kinds of logins:
- **Admin** — everything, including payments, prices and exports.
- **Editor** — event details, FAQs, announcements, sponsors and the inbox only.

## Opening registrations
1. **Races & pricing** → for each race enter the **Entry fee (₹)** and **Capacity**.
2. Change **Registration** to **Open** and press **Save**. (A race can't be opened until fee and capacity are set.)
3. The website updates within about a minute. The race cards switch to "Register" buttons.

Other options per race: flag-off time, cut-off time, minimum/maximum age (checked against the date of birth on race day), start/finish point, elevation and a route-map image link.
- **Paused** — temporarily stops new registrations.
- **Closed** — registrations finished.
- Registrations stop automatically when a race is **full**.

## Updating event details
**Event details** → fill in the race date, start point, reporting time, bib collection, hydration, medical, parking, race kit and awards.
- Anything left empty shows "To be announced" on the website — only fill in confirmed information.
- Adding the **race date** starts the countdown on the event page and lets Google show the event in search results.

## Announcements, FAQs, sponsors
**FAQs, updates & sponsors**:
- **Announcements** — the newest one appears as "Latest update" on the event page.
- **FAQs** — edit, reorder (lower number = higher up), hide or add.
- **Sponsors** — add name, level and a logo link. They appear on the homepage, event page and partner page.

## Participants
**Participants** lets you search by name, mobile, email or registration ID, and filter by race, payment status and date.
- **Export this list** downloads exactly what you see as a CSV file that opens in Excel.
- Click a name to see full details, correct mistakes (name, email, mobile, T-shirt, emergency contact), add internal notes, **resend the confirmation email**, or **cancel** a registration.
- Payment statuses: **Paid** (verified by Razorpay), **Pending** (the bank hasn't confirmed yet — the place is held for 30 minutes), **Failed**, **Cancelled**.
- Registrations can only be marked **Paid** by Razorpay, never by hand. **Refunds** are made in the Razorpay dashboard.

## Inbox
Contact-form messages, partnership enquiries and the newsletter sign-up list (download as CSV). Click **Mark as handled** when you've replied.

## Audit log
Every important change (prices, capacity, cancellations, exports, edits) is recorded with who did it and when.

## Text that isn't in the admin area
A few texts are in the code and need your hosting person to change them:
- The founder story on the About page.
- The legal pages (privacy, terms, waiver, refunds). **These are drafts — have a lawyer review them before opening registrations.**
- The phone number, email and Instagram shown on the site (these are settings in Vercel).
